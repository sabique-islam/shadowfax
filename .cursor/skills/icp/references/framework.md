# ICP framework

**"Developers" is not an ICP.** Role, company shape, trigger, why they move first.

**Pick cluster most likely to try unfinished product:** pain now, reachable, can act without committee if true.

**Who says no:** name not-ICP this cycle (too slow, no pain, wrong stage).

**TAB composition:** one persona per touchpoint; users and buyers may differ.

Sources: complaint clusters, paid substitutes, hacky workarounds, interview buckets from `talk-to-users`.

After ICP: `positioning`, then `distribution` or `first-users`.
