# ICP framework

**"Developers" is not an ICP.** Role, company shape, trigger, why they move first.

**Pick cluster most likely to try unfinished product:** pain now, reachable, can act without committee if true.

**Who says no:** name not-ICP this cycle (too slow, no pain, wrong stage).

**TAB composition:** one persona per touchpoint; users and buyers may differ.

Sources: complaint clusters, paid substitutes, hacky workarounds, interview buckets from `talk-to-users`.

After ICP: `positioning`, then `distribution` or `first-users`.

## Find similar customers from the reason they stay

Start with customers who reached repeat value or paid for the recurring job, and record why they stayed. Compare with nonconverters or customers who left. A familiar logo, funding stage, or a single enthusiastic user is not enough to define the next audience.

Derive a few testable fit rules from workflow, constraints, substitute, recurring trigger, and ability to try. Keep observed traits separate from the explanation of why they matter. Test those rules against a small new set and deliberately include counterexamples. Do not claim a lookalike model was trained when you only made a shortlist.

A post liker is not a lookalike customer. A relevant hiring signal may improve timing research but does not establish budget. Name exclusions and unresolved buying-role questions before expanding the list. If a single outlier customer supplied every rule, label that limitation and seek contrary evidence.
