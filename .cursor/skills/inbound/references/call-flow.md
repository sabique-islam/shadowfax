# Inbound call flow

## Open (30 sec)

Who they are, why they reached out, confirm time.

## Discovery (70% listen)

- What triggered this now?
- What do you use today?
- What does the pain cost (time, money, risk)?
- Who else cares internally?
- Who holds budget?

## Demo

Their data, their job, their win (`activation` path). Not every feature.

## Close

Specific action + calendar date. "What would you need to be sure?"

## Four-part deal check

Champion, buyer, pain with cost, trigger. Missing one → not a deal yet.

## Follow-up email

Reference their words. One ask. Plain text.

Pair with `sales` for objection depth and `buyer-gap` for champion packet.
