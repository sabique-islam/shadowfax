# Pre-delivery

Scan the draft. Clusters only. Isolated hits the linter already dropped.

- Throat-clearing or "here's what" frames: delete, state the thing
- Copula dodge ("serves as"): is/has
- Inanimate actor ("the decision emerges"): name the person
- Forced three, "not X but Y": say the point
- Chatbot residue: cut
- Em dash / en dash: period or comma, unless the founder's sample uses them
- Three sentences the same length: break one
- A fact, metric, quote, trend, or lived anecdote that is not in the source: remove or turn into a question
- Email/DM still has markdown headers: strip them
- Fake "in the trenches" / "excited to announce" / "people don't realize": cut or replace with a sourced actor
- After the pass the writer sounds like a different person, or like a stack of slogans: undo the personality inject
- Authenticity: would the founder say this out loud in this channel? If no, another pass
