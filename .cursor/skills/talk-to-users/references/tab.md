# TAB system (Frankl)

**Composition:** one+ persona per touchpoint; users and buyers; target 30–50 members.

**Cadence:** 30 min, 1:1, monthly, ~6 months; same questions; recorded with permission; never pitch.

**Recruitment:** personalize; lead with their problem; max 4 sentences; ~10% reply → plan ~500 outreaches for ~50; early access not payment.

## Seven questions (30 min max)

1. Magic wand: change anything about how you do [task]?
2. Stakes: how would that change your day?
3. Why now: what's different vs five years ago?
4. What caused this in your org?
5. What have you tried, why did it fall short?
6. What if nothing changes for a year?
7. Where do you go to stay current? (channel discovery)

## Buckets (exact user sentences)

| Bucket | Mine for |
|---|---|
| Pains | bad outcomes, risks, obstacles |
| Gains | outcomes they want |
| Jobs | what they're accomplishing |
| Environmental changes | trends → villain |

Feed `positioning`, `value-prop`, `homepage`.
