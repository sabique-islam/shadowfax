# Choose the shortest credible path to a signal

A tactic is how you earn attention inside a channel. A free tool is an asset; it still needs distribution. A referral is a mechanism; it still needs satisfied users and eligible recipients. Pick the bottleneck before choosing either.

| Evidence / constraint | Plausible path to test | Evidence needed before choosing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A few identifiable people with an urgent job | Specific email or community reply | Named artifacts, reachable people, a useful offer |
| Recurring questions in a trusted room | Answer, teardown, or useful template | Actual threads and room permission |
| Repeated searched diagnostic task | Free tool or specific useful resource through search | Current queries, alternatives, buyer bridge, maintenance capacity |
| Buyers trust a niche expert or publisher | Small partnership | Audience overlap and concrete deliverable |
| Retained users already share work | Product sharing/collaboration or referral | Real sender and recipient value, observed repeat usage |
| A product needs capacity-limited onboarding | Qualified waitlist through a chosen launch surface | Honest constraint and invitation follow-through |
| Existing users miss first value or stop recurring work | Activation or lifecycle work | Actual behavior and a fix; more acquisition may be irrelevant |

Compare time to a meaningful conversation or first value, founder hours, cash/maintenance cost, audience specificity, observability, and ability to repeat. Do not turn ordinal scores into predicted ROI. A slow search strategy may be useful later but poorly matched to a founder who needs five buyer conversations this week.

For one selected path, document where the ICP already is, the specific placement/contact, the useful artifact or offer, minimum resources, expected signal date, and why the next plausible alternative loses under these constraints. The threshold is a proposed decision rule, not a borrowed industry benchmark.

Name a single next specialist: `free-tools`, `partnerships`, `referrals`, `waitlist`, `brand-replies`, `growth-hacks`, `outreach`, or another existing skill. Do not run an entire campaign suite because this reference lists it.
