# Market to devs, sell to buyers

**User:** adopts, feels hours saved (Chronos).
**Buyer:** approves budget for outcome (Kairos): release cycle back, incident avoided, compliance, competitive win.

**Champion:** dev who loves it and fights internally.

**Middle-out:** arm champion with one paragraph, number, and "why now" for their boss.

**Multithread:** deal with only champion is fragile; reach budget holder.

**Triggers:** incident, audit deadline, scaling pain, security review, exec mandate.

Pair with `pricing` (team/SSO/compliance line) and `sales` (discovery, demo their use case).
