# Review standard

- **Specific, not puffery.** No powerful, seamless, best-in-class, platform, revolutionary. Number, name, or proof, or cut.
- **Developer is the hero**, not the product.
- **Speaks to real ICP** from run-state, not "developers."
- **Validated facts only** for load-bearing claims. Flag `[assumption]` out loud.
- **Skeptical dev test:** could they falsify this in 30 seconds?
- **Human voice.** No em dashes unless founder sample uses them.

By deliverable:

- **Positioning:** problem-first, villain, survives "every competitor could say this."
- **Value prop:** real job-to-be-done, dev would repeat in their words.
- **Homepage:** for the developer who visits; first value obvious.
- **Launch:** honest, problem-first, would not get flamed.
- **Pricing:** value metric, buyer can defend to budget holder.
- **Sales:** four-part deal test; friendly free user ≠ buyer.
- **Roadmap:** diagnosis + Now/Next/Later, not a copy of product truth.

## Growth and social deliverables

- Mechanism: explain why this audience would take the next useful action, not just why a stunt attracts attention.
- Feasibility: named placement, actual capability, resource ceiling, owner, and manual pilot before expansion.
- Evidence: source supports the precise metric/unit/window. Self-reported cases and teaching examples stay labeled.
- Economics: contribution rather than gross revenue; cost per actual activated/paying user; reward liability capped.
- Measurement: sample unit, denominator, observation window, comparison limits, and a chosen stopping rule.
- Brand replies: verify the specific thread, voice, language, audience fit, and whether support is needed first. Original wit is allowed; invented context and copied brand lines are not.
- Decision: one next action and an honest no-go option. Do not claim a campaign worked from attention alone.
