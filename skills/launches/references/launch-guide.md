# A launch that produces usable evidence

## Start with the primary room

The channel must be chosen from audience evidence. The default is one primary room. Add a second only when the founder explicitly chose it and can service it; reuse the same product truth rather than inventing a new positioning for each network.

A working product is not enough if a stranger cannot get first value. Rehearse with a few target users: can they understand the job, try the sample, recover from errors, and reach the promised result? A landing page with no working experience may be an honest waitlist announcement, but it is not every kind of launch.

## Prepare the artifact and the measurement

- One primary promise, supported by a working example and explicit limits.
- A demo or synthetic sample a stranger can use. Describe required signup/payment honestly.
- A relevant first-value action and event definition; unique visitors are the denominator, internal tests excluded.
- Source-specific tracking, a window for return/purchase, and a way to capture qualitative feedback without collecting private inputs in analytics.
- Founder availability to answer questions, a support path, and a response if the product fails under load.
- A bounded effort/spend ceiling and a plan for what happens to interested users after the launch.

## Illustrative schedule, adjust to the room

Seven days before: verify current platform rules, rehearse the demo, and choose a customer outcome to watch. Three days before: prepare post, visuals, truthful objection replies, and event tracking. Day before: test links and activation again; prepare a fallback if a dependency fails. Launch: founder posts and stays present for useful replies. Next day: diagnose where relevant visitors dropped off. After one or two natural use intervals: assess return/purchase and record the next decision.

These are preparation intervals, not a claim about the best launch weekday or algorithm. Use the founder's timezone and capacity. Do not fabricate a countdown merely to create urgency.

## Room-specific requirements

[Show HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) ask for something people can try, ideally without signup barriers; signup pages and newsletters alone do not qualify. The founder must have worked on it and be available to discuss it. Do not ask friends to vote or comment. Recheck current rules at execution.

[Product Hunt's current promotion guide](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734384-5-how-to-promote-your-launch-and-what-to-avoid) prohibits upvote solicitation, purchased engagement, and giveaways tied to votes. Draft an invitation to try the product and give feedback. No vote-exchange plan or famous-hunter dependency.

For a subreddit, Discord server, newsletter, or marketplace, inspect that exact room's current rules and format. A generic Reddit strategy cannot establish moderator permission for a particular post.

## Objection bank

| Reader question | Draft from |
| --- | --- |
| Why not the existing alternative? | Tested difference for this job plus where that alternative is better |
| Is this just a wrapper? | Actual workflow value, integration, or outcome evidence |
| Can I trust my data here? | Current processing/storage behavior, limits, and real policy link |
| What happens when the sample is larger? | Measured test conditions or an honest unknown |
| Is the price worth it? | Specific value metric and buyer-relevant tradeoff |
| Your demo broke | Acknowledge observed failure, real recovery path, no invented fix ETA |

## Interpret results

Impressions without relevant visits: audience/message mismatch. Visits without starts: promise/friction. Starts without first value: activation. First value without return: recurrence/fit. Return without payment: value metric, buyer, or package. No exposure may be a distribution failure rather than product rejection. Do not treat product-of-the-day as the business outcome.

Write a short postmortem with dated counts, denominators, limitations, observed objections, and one next decision. The launch is a test; don't keep producing promotion when the evidence points to activation.
