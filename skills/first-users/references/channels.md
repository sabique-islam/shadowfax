# First 50 users

**DREAM top (Frankl):** Discovery = unique humans; Research = newsletter/Discord/follow before they forget you.

**Pre-PMF channels (Czakon):** people who posted the problem; TAB → design partners; founder useful posts in ICP rooms; one deep launch.

**Post-PMF later:** content/SEO, community, influencers, paid to amplify what converts.

## Channel pick

```
ICP concentrated (subreddit, Discord, conference, tag)?
├─ YES → all-in there first
└─ NO  → ICP too broad → tighten `icp`
```

## Mistakes

Post more without thesis. Paid pre-PMF. Community graveyard at 3 lurkers. Ten shallow launches. Optimize acquisition while activation leaks.
