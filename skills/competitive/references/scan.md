# Market scan cadence

Re-check rivals when: rebrand, repricing, new entrant, category rename, or brief older than a quarter.

Per rival: rebrand? repositioning line changed? pricing shift?

Update **Evidence** with dated deltas. If positioning must move, note in **Last observation** and run `roadmap` to reprioritize.

Do not produce spreadsheet theater. Two or three deltas that matter beat twenty rows.
