# Disposition before persuasion

## Read the current message

Separate new text from quoted history, disclaimers, and signatures. A quoted “unsubscribe” in an earlier email or a product discussion about unsubscribe buttons is not necessarily the current sender's removal request. Conversely, an unambiguous request in any language is a stop even if a keyword list misses it. If language or intent is uncertain, hold the account and ask for interpretation rather than guessing a favorable sales label.

| Actual meaning | Next action | Draft behavior | Measurement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opt-out / remove my information | Suppress/hold; founder updates external sender too | No pitch or clarifying sales ask; minimal factual acknowledgment only if appropriate | Removal request, never an objection win |
| Clear no / not interested | Close cold outreach | Usually no response, or acknowledge without a question | Decline |
| Not now, with a real condition/date | Hold and record the condition | Confirm a proposed next step only if needed and welcomed | Timing condition, not pipeline revenue |
| Automatic OOO | Hold for review of the return context | Do not reply to an autoresponder | Not a human reply |
| Wrong person / named referral | Verify who was named and what was permitted | Thank briefly if useful; do not invent a warm introduction | Referral, not qualification |
| A factual question | Answer the asked question with known facts | One relevant answer and optional small next step | Question; qualification remains unknown |
| A concrete objection | Understand the constraint and answer honestly | Evidence if available; accept nonfit without inventing ROI | Objection; no automatic opportunity |
| Clear interest | Help them perform a relevant next step | A real trial path, useful example, or scheduling proposal grounded in actual availability | Interest; track qualification separately |
| Unknown / sensitive / technical assurance | Hold and escalate to the founder | State what can be answered; leave unsupported promises out | Unknown |

No automatic inference that a long response is strong intent, a short response is a brush-off, or a budget objection hides some other concern. The stated constraint may be exactly right.

## Original hypothetical examples

These are teaching cases, not actual customer replies or campaign results.

“Remove me. We already have a vendor.” → opt-out takes precedence. Do not reply with a comparison or ask what the vendor lacks. Record the removal and tell the founder to update any external sequencer.

“What's the monthly price?” → answer the actual published price and conditions. If product truth says $40/month for one workspace with usage charged separately, disclose both. Do not dodge with a meeting ask or fabricate a discount. If pricing is absent, prepare a question for the founder before a final response.

“Our current contract ends in March.” → a factual timing condition. Preserve the year/timezone if known and the contract constraint; do not interpret it as permission for monthly reminders. Ask about a later discussion only if appropriate in the relationship.

“I don't handle this; Priya owns imports.” → referral. Verify Priya's role/source and whether a forward or introduction was offered. “Alex introduced us” is unsupported unless an introduction actually occurred.

“Does this support attachments?” → answer tested support and limits. If only CSV fields are supported, say so plainly. No promise to ship attachments next week without a real commitment.

“We tried it and the preview errors on column two.” → product help takes precedence over a new sales pitch. Ask for the minimal synthetic/redacted reproduction and provide a real support path. Do not request their full private export by default.

“Stop is a button name in our integration. Can it be renamed?” → this is a product question, not necessarily an opt-out. Read the whole message. If uncertain, hold rather than act on a single word.

“हाँ, कृपया मुझे अपनी सूची से हटा दें।” → a clear removal request even though it is not English. Respect the meaning; do not rely on an English-only classifier.

## Ground the answer

Before drafting: what did they ask, what is known, what source supports the answer, what is the smallest useful next step, and is any response wanted? Keep a short claim map for price, capability, deadline, and proof. Use actual prior promises; no fake “as discussed.” A sales technique cannot make an unknown customer case study exist.

The local ledger records all replies as an account hold, including OOO and referrals. The founder can continue an appropriate conversation manually. There is no command that automatically restarts a cold sequence. This is a conservative review workflow, not a mailbox service.
