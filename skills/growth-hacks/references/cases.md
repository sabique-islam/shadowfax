| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## A source discrepancy worth preserving

Swiggy's agency page reports a 2,100% Instagram-to-platform traffic spike, while the [One Show award submission](https://www.oneclub.org/awards/theoneshow/-award/37251/voice-of-hunger/) gives a 2,400% app-traffic increase from social. Scope or reporting may differ. Do not choose the larger number, merge the two, or present either as an independently measured incremental effect.

## How to add a case

Capture source owner, publication/event dates when available, mechanism, audience/resources, exact reported unit and time window, evidence type (artifact, first-party report, independent study), attribution limits, and failure conditions for transfer. A repost of the same press release is not independent corroboration. A source proves what it states, not an unstated conversion rate. If the link disappears, label the claim unverified until another original can be found.

An adaptation is a new hypothesis. Record its outcome separately from the historical example. Never label the founder's planned campaign validated because a brand once used a similar idea.
